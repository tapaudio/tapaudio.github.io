## StudioDeck

**StudioDeck: A Precision Audio Workbench**

StudioDeck is a deck-style audio workbench. Import a song, switch between the Pitch, Stems and Trim decks, and every adjustment — key, tempo, stem levels, clip range — is previewed live and baked into the exported file exactly as you heard it.

**Key Features:**

1. **Pitch Deck:** Semitone and cent-level transpose plus speed control, with derived key and BPM readouts.
2. **Stems Deck:** Cloud separation into six stems; per-track mute, solo and fader mixing; one-tap export of your accompaniment mix.
3. **Trim Deck:** Drag clip edges on the waveform, nudge with precise steps, audition the selection, and export the cut.
4. **Transform Awareness:** Export from any deck warns you when live pitch/tempo transforms will be baked in — no surprises in the output file.
5. **Audio Toolbox:** Direct tools for AB loop, fades, silence removal, format conversion, volume and channel processing, BPM and key analysis.
6. **Practice Guidance:** Contextual coach marks and a help center that can load a track for you and replay guidance on demand.

**Who is it for:**

Musicians and producers who want quick, accurate audio edits on-device, teachers preparing stems and clips for lessons, and practice-focused learners who live on the transport controls.

Applicable scene:
Stem mixing for practice, vocal-removal backing tracks, precise clip trimming, key/tempo preparation before rehearsal.
