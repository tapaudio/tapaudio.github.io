## MusicRoom

**MusicRoom: Your Practice Workbench**

MusicRoom turns any song into material you can practice with. Import a track, slow it down without changing pitch, transpose it to your range, remove the vocals to make your own backing track, loop the hard section — then export the result, exactly as you heard it.

**Key Features:**

1. **Guided Practice Wizards:** Five four-step scenes — slow practice, transpose & sing, make-a-backing-track, cut & splice, song analysis — walk you through every job.
2. **Pitch & Speed:** ±6 semitones transpose and 50–100% speed control with real-time preview; what you hear is what exports.
3. **Stem Separation:** Cloud separation into six stems (vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano, other) with a full mixer and adjustable vocal keep.
4. **Loop & Section Tools:** Drag A–B handles on the waveform, nudge with 0.1s–1s steps, or jump to song sections detected automatically.
5. **Cut & Splice:** Pick two segments — from one song or two — audition each, then join them into a new track.
6. **Audio Toolbox:** 15+ direct tools including AB loop, trim, fade in/out, silence removal, format conversion, volume and channel adjustment, BPM and key detection.

**Who is it for:**

Instrument learners and music students, teachers preparing practice materials for classes, cover musicians building backing tracks, and anyone who needs practical audio edits without a DAW.

Applicable scene:
Slow-down practice, vocal-range transposing, backing-track making, clip splicing for mashups, classroom teaching material prep.
