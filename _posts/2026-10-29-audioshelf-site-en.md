## AudioShelf

AudioShelf turns your e-books into audio you can listen to anywhere — a personal audiobook shelf in your pocket.

Key Features:
	•	E-book to Audio: Convert e-books into spoken audio for listening on the go.
	•	Personal Library: Organize your converted audiobooks on one shelf.
	•	Background Playback: Keep listening with the screen off or while using other apps.
	•	Playback Progress: Pick up exactly where you left off.
	•	File Sharing: Import books and export audio files with ease.

Whether you are commuting, exercising, or resting your eyes, AudioShelf lets your books speak for themselves.
